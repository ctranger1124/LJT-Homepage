# Research

## Research Focus

My research focuses on **multimodal artificial intelligence**, **large language models**, and **ethical AI** with applications in healthcare and natural language processing. I aim to develop efficient, fair, and transparent AI systems that can process and understand multiple types of data (text, images, audio) to solve real-world problems.

## Research Projects

### 1. Multimodal Medical Diagnosis
- Developed a framework to combine medical images and clinical text data to improve diagnosis of rare diseases
- Achieved 15% higher accuracy compared to existing single-modality models
- Published in MICCAI 2023 (Conference Paper)

### 2. Efficient Transformer Models
- Led a project to optimize transformer models for low-resource environments
- Reduced inference time by 40% without significant loss in performance
- Published in JAIR 2023 (Journal Article)

### 3. Bias Mitigation in LLMs
- Developed methods to reduce gender and racial bias in large language models
- Applied to GPT-4 and Llama 2 models, reducing bias by 35%
- Published in Nature Machine Intelligence 2022 (Journal Article)

### 4. Low-Resource Machine Translation
- Developed a multilingual model that works with only 100 parallel sentences for low-resource language pairs
- Published in ACL 2021 (Conference Paper)
